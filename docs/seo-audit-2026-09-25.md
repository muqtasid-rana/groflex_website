# Groflex SEO and GEO audit (25 Sep 2026)

One thing first: no one can put a site at the top of every search engine for every query, and no one can make AI assistants recommend it on demand. Anyone who promises that is selling something. What does work is making Google and the AI tools read Groflex clearly as "a white-label team for UK/US agencies", and then building enough outside proof that they're comfortable recommending you.

Right now four things stand in the way:
- A code bug is hiding most of your pages from Google.
- Several other companies share the Groflex name.
- Search engines still describe you with your old positioning.
- There is almost no outside proof, such as reviews and mentions, that AI tools rely on.

The first can be fixed in a day; the others take months.

## 1. Critical issues (fix this week)

**1.1 Most of your pages tell Google "I'm a copy of the homepage."**
[src/app/layout.js:26-28](../src/app/layout.js#L26-L28) sets the canonical tag to `/` for the whole site. Any page that doesn't set its own inherits it. I checked the live site. These pages all point at `https://www.groflex.co`:
- `/blog`
- every blog post
- `/case-study/2` to `/6`
- `/terms` and `/privacy-policy`

Google treats them as duplicates of the homepage and mostly won't index them. That makes your blog worthless for SEO right now.

**1.2 Your sitemap and robots.txt use the wrong domain.**
Your canonical domain is `www.groflex.co`. But [sitemap.js](../src/app/sitemap.js) and [robots.js](../src/app/robots.js) fall back to `https://groflex.co`, so every sitemap URL is a redirect. Two more problems:
- `groflex.co → www` is a **307 (temporary)** redirect. It should be a 308 (permanent).
- `http://groflex.co` takes two hops: 308 to https, then 307 to www.

**1.3 Search engines still have your old positioning.**
Search results still show *"Groflex — Software & Design Agency… for enterprise clients"*. When I asked an AI about groflex.co as a white-label agency, it answered that you "focus on delivering your own branded services rather than white label". Your new pages haven't been re-crawled or re-read yet.

**1.4 Every page lists the same fake "last updated" date.**
The sitemap stamps every URL with `lastModified: new Date()`, so every page claims it changed today. Google learns to ignore your dates.

## 3. On-page and keyword targeting

- **Homepage H1** is "Your extension to quality work without increasing headcount". It contains no search term, and the H2s ("How it works", "Our pricing") don't either. The page title is fine.
- **There are no dedicated service pages.** Agencies search for specific things, and a single homepage can't rank for all of them. Each of these needs its own page:
  - "white label web development"
  - "white label app development"
  - "white label WordPress development"
  - "white label UI/UX design"
  - "white label SEO"
  - "white label automation / n8n": low competition, a real opening for you
- **There are no UK/US pages.** Searches like "white label agency UK" and "white label development partner USA" have different intent. A UK page can show GBP pricing, UK time overlap and invoicing details. A US page can do the same for USD and US time.
- **/pricing** has the H1 "Pricing Plans" and the title "Pricing — Groflex". It should target "white label agency pricing". Your real, specific prices are a big advantage for AI answers, so put them front and centre.
- **The blog** has two posts, both about small-business websites converting. None are written for agency owners.

## 4. Brand identity (why searching "Groflex" doesn't find you)

When I searched "Groflex", **groflex.co was not on the first page**. Other companies own the name:
- grofleX invoicing software (Capterra, GetApp, Tracxn)
- groflex.ai (San Francisco)
- groflextech.com
- groflexerp.com

The LinkedIn result that comes up is `groflexai`, not you.

Your own signals disagree with each other:
- One founder profile says "Groflex Technologies". Another lists Hubbaish Rana as "Founder & CEO".
- The schema links `instagram.com/groflex.co`, but search indexes `@groflex_co`.
- The schema's `sameAs` includes a WhatsApp link, which isn't a profile.
- The schema logo is the favicon, and the only address and phone are in Pakistan.

To Google and to AI models, "Groflex" is a vague name spread across five companies. You win it back by repeating **one identical description everywhere**, backed by structured data that points at the same set of profiles.

## 5. Outside authority

For "best white label agency UK/US", every AI tool and Google builds its answer from:
- "Top 10 white label agencies 2026" lists (Zestcode, Wildnet, Kiwicommerce, eco-n-tech, OneLittleWeb, Plain English)
- Clutch reviews: the competitors named have **80 to 500+ reviews**

Groflex is on none of these lists and has no review profile I could find. **This is the biggest long-term gap, and code can't fix it.**

## 6. GEO: AI visibility

AI tools recommend a company when three things hold:
1. Several independent sources describe it the same way.
2. Its own site answers the question directly, with specifics such as prices, turnaround and process.
3. It shows up in the pages the AI retrieves, which are listicles, Reddit, Clutch and comparison posts.

Today Groflex has none of the three. You do have two unusual, quotable facts that AI tools can repeat:
- "Pilot: $0 upfront, pay $750 only if you like the work"
- Published per-credit pricing

Most competitors hide their prices, and AI tools favour concrete numbers.
